# 01 — Brand Identity

> **Project:** A premium digital platform for self-confidence, identity transformation, emotional mastery, mindset development, and internal-dialogue change.
> **Design ambition:** Apple / Notion / Linear / Framer calibre. Not a coaching website — a product you *inhabit*.
> **Recommended brand name (working):** **SELV** — see §12, Naming.

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy

**Core belief: Confidence is not a feeling. It is an architecture.**

Most self-improvement brands sell emotion — hype, motivation, a temporary high. This brand sells *structure*. Our philosophy rests on four pillars:

1. **Identity precedes behavior.** People don't fail at habits; they fail at identity. You cannot consistently act like someone you don't believe you are. Every product decision starts from "who is the user becoming," never "what should the user do."
2. **The inner voice is an interface.** Your internal dialogue is the operating system through which you experience everything. It can be inspected, refactored, and redesigned — deliberately, like software.
3. **Calm is credible.** Transformation brands shout. We whisper. Restraint signals confidence; the brand must *embody* the very quality it teaches. No urgency tactics, no exclamation marks, no before/after theatrics.
4. **Evidence over inspiration.** Every method is grounded in behavioral psychology and cognitive neuroscience (self-affirmation theory, cognitive restructuring, identity-based habit formation, neuroplasticity). Inspiration decays in hours; mechanisms compound for life.

**The philosophical one-liner:** *"You are not broken. You are unbuilt."*

## 2. Mission

> **To give every person the tools, language, and daily practice to rebuild their identity from the inside out — turning self-confidence from a personality trait into a learnable skill.**

Operational translation: ship a digital environment where identity work feels as precise, satisfying, and habitual as using a world-class productivity tool.

## 3. Vision

> **A world where inner architecture is treated with the same seriousness as physical health — where "I'm working on my self-concept" is as normal as "I'm going to the gym."**

10-year picture: the platform is the default reference for evidence-based confidence work; its vocabulary ("inner dialogue audit," "identity ledger," "confidence reps") has entered common language.

## 6. Tone of Voice

### Principles

1. **Speak to the person they're becoming, not the person they fear they are.** Never "Struggling with low self-esteem?" Always "The version of you who walks into any room — this is where they're built."
2. **Second person, present tense.** "You notice the thought. You name it. You choose the next one."
3. **Short sentences carry weight. Long sentences carry nuance.** Alternate deliberately.
4. **Verbs over adjectives.** Not "amazing transformation" — "rebuild," "rewire," "author," "audit."
5. **Never pathologize.** Banned words: *broken, fix, hack, crush, grind, unstoppable, alpha, 10x, secret, guru*. Preferred: *build, practice, rehearse, author, evidence, rep, architecture, dialogue*.
6. **Numbers are honest.** "12 minutes a day" beats "just minutes a day."

### Voice by context

| Context | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hero / manifesto | Poetic, declarative | "The voice in your head was written by other people. Take the pen back." |
| Product UI | Precise, quiet | "Session saved. Streak: 12 days. Next: Evening reflection, 6 min." |
| Educational | Clear, mechanistic | "Cognitive reappraisal works because naming an emotion reduces amygdala activation — the brain literally quiets when you label the feeling." |
| Errors / empty states | Kind, blame-free | "Nothing here yet. That's not a gap — it's a beginning." |
| Emails | Editorial, personal | Written like a letter from the mentor, never like a newsletter blast. |

## 8. Target Audience Personas (Brand-level)

*(Full UX personas with goals, behaviors, and journey maps live in `02-ux-strategy.md` §1. These are the brand-strategy summaries.)*

1. **The High-Functioning Doubter** — 28–42, professionally successful, privately runs on impostor syndrome. Reads deeply, distrusts hype, would never call a "life coach." Willing to pay premium for evidence and taste. *Primary persona.*
2. **The Rebuilder** — 30–55, post-rupture (divorce, layoff, burnout, relocation). Identity feels demolished; needs structure and gentleness in equal measure.
3. **The Quiet Ascender** — 22–30, early career, introverted, capable. Wants presence and voice without becoming someone they're not.
4. **The Plateaued Achiever** — 35–50, hit every external milestone, feels internally hollow. Seeks meaning-level work, not tactics.

Shared traits: design-literate, privacy-conscious, allergic to guru culture, already users of Notion/Linear/Things/Arc-class products.

## 9. Customer Journey (Brand narrative arc)

Five acts, mapped to the emotional truth of each stage:

| Act | Stage | User's inner state | Brand's job | Key touchpoints |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | **Recognition** | "This describes me — and no one else has said it this way." | Mirror, don't sell. | Essay/podcast content, the manifesto page, word of mouth. |
| II | **Trust** | "This is serious. This isn't a guru." | Show mechanism + taste. | Method page, free Inner Dialogue Audit, library previews. |
| III | **Threshold** | "Can I actually do this?" | Lower the first step to 7 minutes. | Free 7-day "First Rep" sequence, pricing transparency. |
| IV | **Practice** | "It's working — quietly." | Make progress visible, private, and honest. | Habit tracker, journal, identity ledger, weekly review ritual. |
| V | **Embodiment** | "I don't need the app daily — I *am* this now." | Celebrate graduation; invite them to give back. | Success stories, community mentorship, alumni layer. |

Note the deliberately anti-SaaS Act V: we design for *graduation*, and say so publicly. This is the single strongest trust signal in the category.

> We built the environment we couldn't find: quiet where the industry is loud, precise where it is vague, private where it is performative. A place to rebuild the self, one sentence at a time.

## 12. Naming

### Recommended: **SELV**

Old Norse / Scandinavian for *"self."* Four letters, one syllable, phonetically solid (the closing "lv" feels carved, sturdy).

- **Why it wins:** minimal like *Arc/Linear/Vercel*; literal meaning is the product; no self-help baggage; works as verb-adjacent noun ("Do your Selv work"); superb wordmark potential (see §13); `.com` variants (`selv.co`, `joinselv.com`, `selv.app`) plausibly attainable.
- **Sub-brand system it unlocks:** *Selv Method™, Selv Academy, The Confidence Lab by Selv, Selv Journal.*
- **Risk to clear:** trademark search in software/education classes; a Scandinavian fintech uses a similar mark in insurance — different class, but verify.

### Shortlist alternatives

| Name | Rationale | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|
| **Statura** | Latin root of "stature" — standing tall. Premium, ownable. | Slightly pharma-sounding. |
| **Innerform** | Inner + form(ation). Descriptive yet elegant. | Longer wordmark. |
| **The Becoming** | Poetic, editorial, article-led like *The Browser*. | Hard to verb; long. |
| **Meridian** | Highest point of an arc; navigational. | Crowded namespace. |
| **Auren** | Invented; warm, golden connotation (aurum). | Meaningless without brand spend. |
| **Rootline** | Depth + lineage + baseline. | Reads slightly agricultural. |
| **Unwritten** | The story isn't finished. | Negative prefix; taken in media. |

**Decision guidance:** if SELV fails clearance, go **Statura** (premium, protectable) or **Innerform** (descriptive, SEO-friendlier).

## 13. Logo Concept

### Wordmark: "SELV" — *The Rising Baseline*

Custom-cut geometric grotesque wordmark, all caps, generous tracking (+8%). The defining gesture: **the crossbar of the "E" and the arm of the "L" sit one optical step higher than standard** — an almost-subliminal upward shift. The mark literally *stands taller* than expected. Weight: between Medium and Semibold; terminals cut at 90° for an engineered feel; the "S" spine slightly opened for optical calm.

### Symbol: *The Ascending Stroke*

A single vertical stroke with a subtle forward lean (2–3°) and a rounded apex, sitting on a short horizontal base — abstracted from: a person standing, a cursor (you write yourself), an "I" (identity), and a rising bar. Drawn on the same grid as the iconography (§14). Works at 16 px favicon size. In motion, the symbol draws itself bottom-to-top in 600 ms (ease-out) — the brand's signature animation.

### Lockups & rules

- Primary: symbol left of wordmark, gap = cap-height × 0.75.
- App icon: symbol alone, centered on Ink-950 (dark) or Bone-50 (light) rounded square.
- Clear space: cap-height on all sides. Minimum wordmark width: 72 px.
- Mono only: Ink-950 on light, Bone-50 on dark, or the Solar accent for celebratory moments. Never gradient-filled, never outlined, never skewed.

## 14. Iconography Style

- **Construction:** 24 × 24 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, rounded caps and joins, 2 px corner radius on rectangles. Outline style only — no filled icons except active-state toggles (fill animates in).
- **Character:** geometric with one humanist exception per icon — a single softened curve or open terminal — echoing "warm rigor." Icons feel drafted, not stamped.
- **Metaphor system:** favor *architecture and writing* metaphors (blueprint, pen, foundation, keystone, ledger, compass) over clichéd self-help metaphors (no lightbulbs, no lotus flowers, no mountain-summit flags, no brains with gears).
- **States:** default 1.5 px stroke at 70% ink; hover 100% ink; active adds Solar accent fill at 12% behind the glyph.
- **Source discipline:** single icon family (custom, or Lucide customized to spec) — never mixed sets.

## 15. Photography Direction

**Concept: "The Quiet Moment Before."** We photograph the second *before* transformation is visible — a person at a window at dawn, hands closing a journal, the pause at a doorway. Never the triumphant summit; always the private threshold.

- **Casting:** real diversity of age (25–60), body, ethnicity, and visible disability. No models who look like fitness influencers. Faces show *composure*, not ecstasy — closed-mouth near-smiles, direct or softly averted gazes.
- **Light:** natural, directional, low-angle (dawn/dusk). Strong single-source window light with deep soft shadows. Never studio-flat, never HDR.
- **Color grade:** desaturated 15–20%, lifted blacks toward Ink-900 (never crushed), warm highlights biased toward Bone/Solar. Skin stays true — the grade lives in the environment, not the person.
- **Composition:** generous negative space on one side (for typography), subjects often ⅓-frame, environmental context visible (interiors with texture: linen, wood, concrete, paper).
- **Absolute bans:** stock-photo laughter-with-salad energy, pointing at whiteboards, meditation-on-mountaintop, handshakes, lens flares, before/after diptychs.

---

## 16. Illustration Direction

**Concept: "Blueprints of the Inner World."** Illustration explains what photography cannot show — thoughts, identity, dialogue — using an architectural-drawing language.

- **Style:** fine-line (1–1.5 px) monoline drawings on the brand grid, resembling elegant technical schematics: an exploded-axonometric "self" under construction, dashed lines for old thought-paths and solid lines for new ones, section-cuts through a head rendered as a building floor plan.
- **Color:** Ink line-work on Bone or Ink backgrounds; one accent only (Solar) marking "the new path" or "the insight" — accent is always *earned*, highlighting the transformation moment in each drawing.
- **Texture:** subtle paper grain (3–4% noise) behind illustrations in editorial contexts; clean vector in product UI.
- **Motion-ready:** every illustration is built in layers so lines can draw on with scroll (see motion spec, `04-ui-design-system.md` §15).
- **Bans:** flat "corporate Memphis" people, gradient blob 3D characters, emoji-style faces, literal brains, puzzle pieces, staircases-to-the-sky.
