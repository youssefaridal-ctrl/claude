# 04 — Frameworks, Mental Models & Systems

> The platform's teachable IP: named models used consistently across articles, courses, and programs. Each model gets a one-screen visual treatment (blueprint illustration style) and a matching exercise in `03-practice-library.md`.

---

## 1. Core Framework: The Selv Method (Audit → Rewrite → Rep)

The master sequence everything hangs on. **Audit:** make the inner voice visible (you can't rewrite what you haven't read). **Rewrite:** replace verdicts with believable, authored sentences (believability 7+, values before traits, your dialect). **Rep:** practice daily until the new voice is the default (spaced repetition for the self; evidence logged as identity). Not linear once learned — a loop run at three speeds: per-moment (seconds), per-day (the routines), per-season (programs and quarterly Deltas).

## 2. The Six Narrators (dialogue taxonomy)

The Perfectionist · The Guard · The Ghost · The Prosecutor · The Pleaser · The Prophet *(definitions in site copy, `01 §Method`)*. Teaching value: pattern recognition beats content analysis — you don't need to argue every thought once you can say "that's the Prophet again." Each narrator has: a core sentence, an original protection, a signature body sensation, a preferred rewrite operation (e.g., the Prosecutor responds to Courtroom/evidence; the Prophet to prediction-vs-actual tracking; the Ghost to graded visibility reps).

## 3. The Ledger and the Accountant (confidence bookkeeping)

Confidence = evidence × appraisal. Life writes entries; an internal accountant books them — and a doubting accountant books wins as external ("luck"), losses as identity, ambiguity as loss. The work is retraining the accountant (because-sentences, discount-stamp catching) and revaluing the asset classes: **kept promises to yourself outrank outcomes** — they're the only entries no discount stamp fits. *(Full treatment: Article 4.)*

## 4. The Bodyguard Model (befriending the critic)

Every critic line = a protection + a prophecy + terrible manners. Strip the manners, decode the protection, retire the prophecy. Sequence: Quote → Attribute → Translate → Reassign (write the guard a new job description). The critic isn't an enemy to defeat; it's outdated security software to renegotiate with. *(Full treatment: Article 1.)*

## 5. The Two Drafts (thought ontology)

Any self-referential thought is either **footage** (what happened), **commentary** (interpretation), or **prophecy** (forecast) — and the mind serves all three as if they were footage. The model's move: before responding to any painful thought, ask *which of the three is this?* Only footage gets automatic credence. Commentary gets the Courtroom. Prophecy gets prediction-vs-actual tracking, where its accuracy record is (reliably, measurably) poor.

## 6. The Threshold Model (where confidence is actually built)

Confidence isn't built in comfort or in trauma — it's built at **thresholds**: moments of chosen, survivable exposure. Each day has ~1–3 natural thresholds (the hand half-raised, the message drafted). The model: *name the day's threshold in the morning, script it (Name-Switch), take it at the smallest brave size, log it as evidence.* Growth = thresholds taken per week, not intensity per threshold. Eight small doors beat one heroic gate.

## 7. The Believability Dial (rewrite calibration)

Every self-statement has a believability rating 0–10. Under 7, the fact-checker rejects it (and may rebound-argue against you). At 9–10, it's usually too safe to move anything. **The working zone is 7–8:** true enough to pass, forward enough to stretch. When a rewrite fails, don't inflate it — *shrink it* until it clears 7. Progress = the same sentence's rating drifting upward over weeks, not grander sentences.

## 8. Weather vs. Climate (emotional time-scales)

Feelings are weather (hours), moods are fronts (days), identity is climate (seasons). Category errors cause most self-misdiagnosis: treating one anxious morning as identity ("I'm an anxious person") or a climate problem as weather ("I just need a good week"). The model assigns tools by timescale — weather gets regulation (sighs, labeling, the 90-second wave), fronts get routines and review, climate gets the Method proper. Never make climate conclusions from weather data, especially at night.

## 9. The Confidence Loop (the habit-building system)

Identity → Threshold → Rep → Evidence → (stronger) Identity. Built with three engineering rules:
1. **Identity first:** every habit is anchored to "I'm someone who…" before it gets a schedule (the Tracker enforces this).
2. **If-then wiring:** implementation intentions ("After [existing cue], I will [2-minute version]") — the most reliable habit-formation tool in the literature.
3. **Grace architecture:** weeks, not chains — ≥4 practice days = a kept week; a missed day is data; "Resumed" is a status, not an apology. Systems that punish lapses select for people who never lapse; ours is built for humans.

## 10. The Repair Model (for when it goes badly)

Confidence isn't never-falling; it's a short repair time. Four steps, in order, timed: **Steady** (body first — sighs, movement; minutes) → **Sort** (footage/commentary/prophecy; that evening) → **Salvage** (one extractable lesson or *dismissed*; same evening) → **Step** (one small re-entry threshold within 72 hours — the deadline matters; avoidance compounds daily). Members learn to measure repair time the way runners measure recovery heart rate — it's the fitness metric of the psyche.

## 11. Success Roadmaps (published as guides)

**Roadmap A — The First 90 Days (from "the voice runs me" to "I run a practice"):**
Weeks 1–2: Audit — transcripts, narrator identified, no rewriting yet (premature rewriting fails). Weeks 3–4: first working sentences at believability 7; morning routine installed at the 3-minute size. Weeks 5–8: full 12-minute mornings + adjournment evenings; one threshold/day; first Fear Ladder completed. Weeks 9–12: Ledger past 60 entries; first Quarterly Delta; repair time measured twice; graduation from the roadmap into maintenance.
**Milestones are behavioral, never emotional** ("ran the routine 5 days" not "felt confident") — feelings lag behavior by weeks, and roadmaps that promise feelings on schedules are lying.

**Roadmap B — The Visibility Ladder (for the Ghost/quiet ascenders):** 8 rungs from "one question in a small meeting" to "presenting an opinion the room may dislike," two weeks per rung, each with scripts, rehearsal reps, and prediction-vs-actual logging.

**Roadmap C — The Rebuild (post-rupture):** stabilization (weather tools only, 2–4 weeks) → inventory (what survived: values, skills, people — the Belief Archaeology applied to "who I was") → drafting (new identity sentences, deliberately provisional) → thresholds (small public acts of the new draft) → consolidation. Explicit gate: acute grief and clinical depression route to professional care first; the roadmap says so on page one.

## 12. Mental Models Index (the vocabulary layer)

Short, named, reusable across all content: **The Fact-Checker** (the mind's claim-auditor; the reason affirmations bounce) · **Discount Stamps** (luck/deflation/disqualification — the accountant's three tools) · **The Docket** (unprocessed charges fuel night court) · **Museum Labels** (attributed thoughts lose authority) · **The Chair and the Window** (observer stance from meditation F1) · **Fuel, Not Verdict** (arousal reinterpretation) · **The 72-Hour Rule** (insight decays in ~3 days unless practiced; avoidance compounds after the same window) · **Doors, Not Gates** (smallest brave thing) · **Done Is Done** (the practice-closing bell; the anti-perfectionism period at the end of every rep).
