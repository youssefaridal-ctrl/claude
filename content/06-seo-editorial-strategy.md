## 4. Internal Linking Strategy

- **Hub-and-spoke:** every supporting article links up to its pillar (first occurrence of head term) and sideways to exactly 2–3 siblings; pillars link down to all spokes via a curated "go deeper" shelf.
- **Content → tool (the conversion lattice):** every article's "Do the rep" block links the one matching Lab tool or exercise; tools link back to their explainer article ("why this works").
- **The vocabulary layer:** first use of any named model (Believability Dial, Discount Stamps, the Docket…) links to its glossary entry in /resources; glossary entries link to the canonical article. This makes our proprietary vocabulary crawlable and citable.
- **Story ↔ method:** every Success Story links the specific exercises named in it; exercises link one story ("see it in someone's life").
- **Hygiene:** 3–6 in-body links per 1,000 words, descriptive anchors only ("the adjournment protocol," never "click here"), quarterly link audit, no orphan pages, breadcrumbs on all Library content.

## 5. Editorial Calendar

**Cadence:** 2 Library articles/week (Tue essay, Thu practical) · The Sunday Rewrite newsletter weekly · 1 podcast episode/fortnight · 1 Success Story/month · pillar refreshes twice yearly · corrections essay (#100) every January.

**Quarterly themes (launch year):**
- **Q1 — Meet the narrator** (Inner Dialogue + Foundations clusters; Audit as CTA everywhere). Ship pillars: self-talk, confidence.
- **Q2 — The rewrite** (Rewrite + Identity clusters; Rewrite Machine featured). Ship pillars: affirmations, limiting beliefs.
- **Q3 — Under pressure** (Thresholds + Emotional Mastery; Rehearsal Room featured; back-to-work September push). Ship pillars: overthinking, speaking up.
- **Q4 — Rebuilding & review** (Rebuilding + Practice; year-end reflection tools; the anti-"new year new you" December essay — our contrarian tentpole: "You don't need a new you. You need a kinder accountant.").

**Per-piece workflow:** brief (target query, reader state, the one idea, mechanism source) → draft → clinical review (accuracy + scope-note check) → edit (voice pass against `00-content-guide`) → accessibility pass (in-brief summary, alt text, reading level) → publish → 90-day performance review (rank, read-through, rep-click rate).

## 7. Reading Recommendations (the honest shelf, for /resources)

**Self-talk & the mind's voice:** Ethan Kross, *Chatter* — the distanced self-talk research, from the researcher · Charles Fernyhough, *The Voices Within* — where inner speech comes from.
**Thought & feeling mechanics:** David Burns, *Feeling Good* — the CBT classic; dated tone, durable tools · Lisa Feldman Barrett, *How Emotions Are Made* — why granularity matters · James Gross (ed.), *Handbook of Emotion Regulation* — for the deep divers.
**Identity & habit:** James Clear, *Atomic Habits* — identity-based habit, popularized well · Timothy Wilson, *Redirect* — story-editing and what actually holds up · Carol Dweck, *Mindset* — read with our honest note on replication debates.
**Self-worth & compassion:** Kristin Neff, *Self-Compassion* — the research case for dropping the whip · Tara Mohr, *Playing Big* — the inner critic in professional life, especially for women.
**The skeptic's shelf (we insist):** Stuart Ritchie, *Science Fictions* — how research overclaims, so you can read everyone (including us) more sharply.
