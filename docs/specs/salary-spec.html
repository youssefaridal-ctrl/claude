01

Module Philosophy

The central premise of Finance Bag is simple and powerful: every monthly salary becomes a complete financial plan the moment it arrives. Before a single dirham is spent, the user has a clear, intentional allocation of where every unit of income goes.

Most budgeting apps treat budgets as retroactive — you spend first, then categorize later. This module inverts that model. The budget is built proactively at the start of each month, and real-world spending is measured against it. The financial plan is the primary artifact; transactions are evidence of execution.

📐
Design Axiom

At any moment, the user should be able to answer five questions without any mental effort: (1) How much did I earn this month? (2) How much have I planned to spend? (3) How much is still unallocated? (4) Am I on track for my financial goals? (5) Is my financial situation getting better or worse? All five answers must be visible on the salary screen without tapping anything.

03

Monthly Budget Cycle

Budget Period Definition

A critical design decision that most budgeting apps get wrong: the budget period should align with when money actually arrives, not with the calendar month. If a user is paid on the 25th of every month, their financial month should run from the 25th to the 24th of the following month.

✅
Salary-Day-Aligned Budget Period

Budget period = from salaryPaymentDay of month N to (salaryPaymentDay − 1) of month N+1. Example: salary paid on the 25th → budget runs 25 Jan → 24 Feb. This matches real cash flow and eliminates the "I'm always broke in the last week of the month" problem that calendar-month budgets create.

Period start
salaryPaymentDay of the current calendar month
Period end
(salaryPaymentDay − 1) of the following calendar month, inclusive
Period ID format
YYYY-MM where MM = the calendar month of the start date. This is the archive key.
Payment day = 29, 30, 31
Capped at 28 in user settings to handle all months (February). System maps day 28 to last day of month when the month has fewer days.
First-time users
First budget period starts from today regardless of salaryPaymentDay. Partial months are valid.

Monthly Lifecycle Stages

💰
Stage 1 — Salary Received
User confirms salary amount (or it is auto-filled from the previous month's amount). Any additional income sources for this month are added. Total Available Income is calculated.
TRIGGER: User action on salary payment day (or push notification reminder)
📋
Stage 2 — Budget Creation
New monthly budget created. Category allocations are pre-populated from the previous month's plan (carry-forward template). User reviews and adjusts. Budget is draft until confirmed.
TRIGGER: Automatic after Stage 1 completion
✅
Stage 3 — Active Month
Budget is active. User adds transactions, tracks spending against allocations, monitors financial health score, receives smart insights. Real-time calculations update as transactions are entered.
TRIGGER: User confirms budget (moves from draft to active)
📊
Stage 4 — End-of-Month Summary
System generates an automatic monthly report 3 days before the budget period ends. Surfaces unspent allocations, final health score, goal progress, and a recommendation for next month's budget. A push notification prompts the user to review.
TRIGGER: Automatic, 3 days before period end date
📦
Stage 5 — Archive
The completed month is snapshotted and archived. A frozen, immutable record of the budget, all categories, allocations, actual spending, health score, and income is stored. The archived month is accessible forever in history.
TRIGGER: Next salaryPaymentDay reached (automatic) or manual "Clôturer le mois" button
🔄
Stage 6 — New Month Starts
Budget template carried forward from archived month. Carry-over balance (if opted in) added to new income. Stage 1 of new cycle begins.
TRIGGER: Immediately after Stage 5

Rollover & Carry-Over Logic

Carry-over is the unspent balance from the previous month. It is opt-in at the category level.

Carry-Over ModeBehaviorUse Case
Off (default)Each month starts fresh. Unspent money is considered "saved" implicitly.Most users. Clean slate each month.
Add to IncomeTotal unspent balance added to next month's Total Available Income as "Reliquat".Users who want to reallocate last month's savings explicitly.
Per-Category RolloverUnspent amount per category carried forward to the same category next month. E.g., unspent "Loisirs" rolls into next month's "Loisirs".Variable categories where spending fluctuates (e.g., clothing budget saved over 3 months for one purchase).
⚠️
Rollover Boundary Rule

Carry-over is calculated only from unspent allocation, never from income itself. If a category was over-spent, its rollover is zero (not negative). Negative rollovers are not applied — debt tracking is handled by the Credits module, not by carry-over.

Income Architecture

Primary Salary

The primary salary is the backbone of the monthly budget. It is a monthly fixed amount that the user declares. The system stores the net salary (after tax and deductions) — we allocate what arrives in the bank account, not the gross figure.

Type
Fixed monthly amount (net)
Currency
User's declared currency from profile
Precision
2 decimal places. Stored as integer centimes (× 100) to avoid float rounding errors.
Minimum
0 (some users may have no salary and rely on other income sources)
Maximum
No upper limit enforced. Validation: warn if value increases by more than 200% vs previous month.
History
Salary changes are timestamped and appended to a salary history log. Previous salaries are never overwritten.
Pre-fill
On new month creation, pre-filled with previous month's salary value. User confirms or adjusts.

Allocation Methods

Percentage-Based
Simple

Each category is assigned a percentage of Total Available Income. Amount adjusts automatically as income changes.

Scales perfectly with salary changes
Easy to understand (50% of income to needs)
Doesn't reflect fixed real-world costs (rent is not % of salary)
Awkward for expenses like subscriptions (€9.99/month)
Fixed-Amount
Precise

Each category has a fixed currency amount. Unallocated remainder is surfaced clearly.

Matches real costs (rent = 3,500 MAD exactly)
Easy to set up when expenses are known
Must be manually updated on income changes
Easy to leave large unallocated remainders
Hybrid ★ RECOMMENDED
Best Fit

Fixed amounts for known costs (rent, loan) + percentage for flexible spending. Remainder shown as allocation runway.

Mirrors how real budgeting works
Fixed costs lock in first, percentages share the rest
Works for any income level and spending pattern
More cognitive load to set up initially
Templates
Quick Start

Pre-built allocation presets that user can apply and then customize.

Zero-friction onboarding for first-time budgeters
Encodes financial wisdom (50/30/20 rule)
One-size-fits-none without customization
Users may apply and never adjust

Hybrid Allocation: Calculation Order

In hybrid mode, the allocation engine processes categories in a strict order to ensure predictable results:

Hybrid Allocation Resolution Order
  1. 1
    Reserve Fixed Amounts
    All categories with allocationType = fixed claim their amounts first. Fixed_Total = Σ(fixed_categories.fixedAmount)
  2. 2
    Calculate Flexible Pool
    Flexible_Pool = Total_Available − Fixed_Total. This is the income remaining after fixed costs.
  3. 3
    Distribute Percentage Categories
    Percentage categories apply their % to Flexible_Pool, not to Total_Available. This ensures percentages reflect the discretionary portion.
  4. 4
    Apply Hybrid Floors
    Categories with allocationType = hybrid get MAX(fixedAmount, percentage × Flexible_Pool). The fixed amount acts as a spending floor.
  5. 5
    Calculate Unallocated Remainder
    Unallocated = Total_Available − Σ(all_categories.computedAmount). Surfaced prominently in the UI.

Real-Time Calculation Engine

Every change to income, allocations, or transactions triggers an instant recalculation cascade. The user must never see stale numbers. All calculations are performed synchronously in the Zustand store layer — no loading indicators, no debounce delays.

Complete Formula Set

Income Calculations
Total_Income = Salary + Σ(income_sources.amount)
Total_Available = Total_Income + Carry_Over
Fixed_Total = Σ(cat.fixedAmount) for cat where allocationType ∈ {fixed, hybrid}
Flexible_Pool = Total_Available − Fixed_Total
Allocation Calculations
Cat.computedAmount = fixedAmount // if type=fixed
Cat.computedAmount = (percentage / 100) × Flexible_Pool // if type=percentage
Cat.computedAmount = MAX(fixedAmount, (pct/100)×Flexible_Pool) // if type=hybrid
Total_Allocated = Σ(cat.computedAmount)
Unallocated = Total_Available − Total_Allocated
Allocation_Pct = (Total_Allocated / Total_Available) × 100
Spending Calculations (current period)
Cat.spent = Σ(tx.amount) for tx where tx.categoryId=cat.id AND tx.type='expense'
Cat.remaining = Cat.computedAmount − Cat.spent
Cat.over_budget = MAX(0, Cat.spent − Cat.computedAmount)
Cat.utilization = (Cat.spent / Cat.computedAmount) × 100 // can exceed 100%
Total_Spent = Σ(cat.spent)
Available_Cash = Total_Available − Total_Spent
Financial Ratios
Savings_Rate = (savings_categories.computedAmount / Total_Income) × 100
Debt_Ratio = (Σ(credits.monthlyPayment) / Total_Income) × 100
Emergency_Progress = (emergencyFund.currentAmount / emergencyFund.targetAmount) × 100
Net_Monthly_Balance = Total_Income − Total_Spent − Σ(credits.monthlyPayment)
Burn_Rate = Total_Spent / days_elapsed × days_in_period
// Burn_Rate projects end-of-month spending at current pace

Smart Insights Engine

The insights engine compares the user's current month data against their 3-month rolling history and their own declared targets. All rules are deterministic — no machine learning at v1.0. A maximum of 3 insights are shown at once, prioritized by severity.

Insight Categories & Trigger Rules

🔴
"Votre budget Loisirs est épuisé à 85% — il reste encore 12 jours."
Alerte Trigger: cat.utilization ≥ 80% AND days_remaining > 7. Priority: High.
📈
"Vos dépenses Alimentation ont augmenté de 23% par rapport à votre moyenne des 3 derniers mois."
Alerte Trigger: cat.spent > avg_3months(cat.spent) × 1.20. Priority: Medium.
💰
"Votre taux d'épargne ce mois-ci est de 22% — votre meilleur score depuis 4 mois !"
Bravo Trigger: savings_rate > max(savings_rate, last_4_months). Priority: Low.
🛡️
"Votre fonds d'urgence est à 67% de l'objectif. Encore 3 mois à ce rythme !"
Progression Trigger: emergency_progress crosses 25%, 50%, 67%, 75%, 90% thresholds. One-time per threshold.
📊
"À ce rythme, vous dépasserez votre budget global le 24 janvier. Réduisez vos dépenses de 340 MAD."
Projection Trigger: Burn_Rate projects Total_Spent > Total_Available before period end. Priority: Critical.
🎯
"Il vous manque 1,200 MAD pour financer votre objectif Voyage ce mois-ci."
Conseil Trigger: goal.monthlyTarget not met by current allocation. Priority: Medium.
✅
"Félicitations ! Vous avez terminé le mois avec un excédent de 620 MAD."
Fin de mois Trigger: End-of-month summary. Net_Monthly_Balance > 0. Priority: Low.
💳
"Votre ratio d'endettement de 38% dépasse la limite recommandée de 30%. Envisagez la méthode Snowball."
Risque Trigger: Debt_Ratio > 30%. Priority: High. Shown once per month max.

Insight Display Rules

  • Maximum 3 insights visible simultaneously in the main UI. Full list accessible via "Voir tout".
  • Insights are ordered by priority: Critical → High → Medium → Low → Praise.
  • Praise/milestone insights are shown for 48 hours maximum, then archived.
  • The same insight is never shown twice in the same period unless the underlying value changes.
  • Insights are written in the user's app language (FR/AR/EN). Values are formatted in the user's currency.
  • Each insight includes a tappable action when applicable (e.g., "Voir la catégorie" or "Ajuster le budget").

Archive Snapshot Contents

An archive is a complete, immutable snapshot. It is never modified after creation. It contains:

period
Start date + end date of the budget period
income
Primary salary + all income sources with amounts and types
total_available
Total income + carry-over at time of archive
categories
Full category list: name, icon, color, allocation method, computedAmount, spent, remaining
transactions
All transactions in the period (description, amount, category, date). Read-only reference.
credits
Snapshot of all credits at period end: names, amounts, payments made
emergency_fund
Balance at end of period, contributions made during period
goals
Each goal's progress at period end + contributions made during period
health_score
Final health score at archive time + component breakdown
carry_over
Unspent balance carried forward to next period (if applicable)
user_note
Optional user-written note for the month (e.g., "Ramadan month"). Added before or at archive time.
archived_at
Timestamp of archive creation. UTC.
tags
User-added tags for the month (e.g., "vacances", "rentrée", "prime")

Archive Storage Architecture

Archives are stored locally in SQLite as a monthly_archives table with a snapshot JSON column. The JSON snapshot is the entire budget state — no relational joins required to reconstruct any historical month. This makes history retrieval O(1) and resistant to future schema changes: the archive is a point-in-time export, not a live database view.

📐
Immutability Rule

Archives are never modified after creation. If a user discovers an error in a past month, they cannot edit the archive. They can add a correction note. Financial history must be an accurate record, not a revisionist one. This is especially important if tax or audit features are added in the future.

Retention Policy

Archives are retained indefinitely on the device. There is no expiration. For v1.0 (local-only storage), the SQLite database on-device is the sole repository. When cloud sync is introduced, archives will be replicated server-side with the same indefinite retention policy.

Monthly Report

Report Structure

The monthly report is generated automatically 3 days before the budget period ends and finalized at archive time. It is a structured summary accessible from the archive view of any completed month.

Monthly Report Sections (in order)

    Header: Period Summary
    Period label, total income, total spent, net balance, and the month's health score grade. The "at a glance" section.
  2. 2
    Income Breakdown
    Primary salary + each income source with amount and type. Total Available Income.
  3. 3
    Budget vs Actual (by category)
    For each category: Allocated · Spent · Remaining · Utilization %. Color-coded (green = under, red = over). Sorted by spent amount descending.
  4. 4
    Savings Summary
    Savings rate, savings categories totals, comparison to previous month and personal goal.
  5. 5
    Debt Payments
    Total monthly payments made, debt ratio, credits remaining amounts.
  6. 6
    Emergency Fund Progress
    Contributions this month, current balance, progress toward target, months to completion at current rate.
  7. 7
    Goals Progress
    Each active goal: contributions this month, total progress, completion date projection.
  8. 8
    Financial Health Score
    Final score with component breakdown. Comparison to previous month. Grade badge.
  9. 9
    Month's Achievements
    Triggered milestones: new savings record, first month under budget, emergency fund milestone, goal completed, etc.
  10. 10
    Recommendations for Next Month
    3 specific, actionable suggestions derived from this month's data. E.g., "Réduisez Loisirs de 200 MAD" or "Augmentez votre contribution fonds d'urgence de 100 MAD".

Report Export

Users can export the monthly report as a PDF (generated on-device using a React Native PDF library). The exported PDF is shareable (e.g., to a financial advisor or family member). Export triggered from the report screen with a share icon.

Skipped month (gap in history) New budget period start is more than 1 period after last archived period Auto-archive the skipped months as empty placeholders with the last known salary and zero spending. Gaps in history are marked as "Mois sans données". "2 mois ont été manqués. Ils ont été archivés avec votre dernier salaire connu."
Late salary salaryPaymentDay passed but user has not confirmed salary for new month Push notification after 2 days. Previous month remains "active" until user manually starts new month or 7 days pass (then auto-archive + auto-start). Notification: "N'oubliez pas de renseigner votre salaire de janvier."
Partial salary User knows they will receive only part of their salary (e.g., mid-month start) User enters the actual received amount. No system override. Health score calculated against this amount. None — user manages this manually.
Unexpected income (bonus received mid-month) User adds an income source after budget is active Total_Available recalculates immediately. Percentage allocations adjust. Fixed allocations unchanged. Unallocated increases to show available windfall. Banner: "Revenu supplémentaire de X MAD ajouté. Votre budget a été mis à jour."
Allocation percentages don't sum to 100% Percentage categories sum to ≠ 100% Allowed. Unallocated shown. Never auto-redistribute — that would override user intent. A nudge suggests closing the gap. "Vos allocations représentent 88% de votre revenu. Il reste 12% non alloués."
All categories deleted categories array = empty Empty state shown on salary screen. CTA to add categories or apply a template. Default categories can be restored from settings. "Aucune catégorie. Ajoutez des catégories ou appliquez un modèle."

Historical archives remain in their original currency with a currency label. "Votre devise a changé. Les montants de ce mois sont maintenant affichés en EUR."

Business Rules Summary

BR-01
One Active Budget Per Period

A user can have exactly one active budget at any time. A period is defined by its start date. No two budgets may have overlapping periods.

BR-02
Amounts in Integer Centimes

All monetary amounts are stored as integers (×100 of the currency unit) to prevent floating-point rounding errors. Display layer converts back to decimals. This rule applies to all modules.

BR-03
Archives Are Immutable

Once a month is archived, its snapshot cannot be modified. Only annotation fields (user_note, tags) can be updated post-archive. Financial history is an accurate record.

BR-04
Computed Amounts Are Never Stored

computedAmount, spent, remaining, Total_Allocated, Unallocated, and all ratios are derived fields. They are never written to the database — they are always calculated at read time. The only persisted allocation values are percentage and fixedAmount. This prevents stale data bugs.

BR-05
Soft Deletes Only

Categories and income sources are never hard-deleted. They are soft-deleted with a deleted_at timestamp. This ensures that past archives referencing deleted categories remain coherent.

BR-06
Negative Balances Are Never Blocked

The app surfaces over-budget states prominently but never prevents the user from saving or continuing. Users are adults. The app informs; it does not gatekeep.

BR-07
Net Salary Only

The app works exclusively with net (take-home) amounts. Gross salary, tax rates, and social contributions are not modeled. We allocate what arrives in the bank account.

BR-08
Salary Payment Day Capped at 28

To handle February and all months uniformly, salaryPaymentDay is capped at 28. Days 29, 30, 31 are mapped to the last day of the month for months where they don't exist.
